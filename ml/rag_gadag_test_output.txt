### 2. Why is "Gadag" Unique? (Palindromic Significance)
According to Section **1.3**, Gadag holds a unique linguistic and geographic distinction:
*   **Exact Palindrome**: In both the Latin script (**G-A-D-A-G**) and Kannada script (**ಗದಗ**), the name reads identically forwards and backwards.
*   **Railway Symmetry**: The official Indian Railways code for Gadag Junction is **"GDG"**, which is also an exact three-letter palindrome.
*   **Ancient Variants**: Historical Western Chalukya inscriptions record names like *Kratuka*, *Galadugu*, and *Gadagu*.

### 3. Tontadarya College of Engineering (TCE) Gadag Overview
Based on Sections **5** through **6**, here are the key details:

*   **Institution Identity**: Established in **1997** by Sri Tontada Siddhalinga Kalyan Kendra (STSKK). It operates under the visionary patronage of His Holiness Dr. Tontada Siddhalinga Mahaswamiji, 19th Pontiff of Jagadguru Tontadarya Sansthan Math.
*   **Accreditations**: Affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), approved by AICTE and DTE Karnataka, and accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA) for several branches. It holds ISO 9001:2015 certification.
*   **Leadership**: Currently led by Principal Dr. M. M. Awati.
*   **Academic Programs**: Offers B.E. degrees in CSE, ECE, EEE (NBA), ME (NBA), CE (NBA), and Agricultural Engineering; plus an MBA program mentioned elsewhere in the full curriculum context. It also hosts recognized Ph.D. research centers for various engineering disciplines.
*   **Location & Connectivity**: Situated on Mundargi Road with easy access via Gadag Junction Railway Station (**Code: GDG**), a Category NSG-4 major junction connecting Hubballi, Hosapete, and Bagalkote.
